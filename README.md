## 📌 Project Overview

The **E-Commerce Sales Analysis** project focuses on analyzing sales data using **MySQL** to understand business performance, customer purchasing behavior, product performance, and revenue trends.

The project uses SQL queries to extract meaningful insights from raw sales data, calculate important business KPIs, identify top-performing products and customers, and analyze geographical sales distribution.

## 🎯 Project Goals

- Analyze overall sales performance and revenue generation.
- Calculate important business KPIs, including total revenue, total orders, successful orders, and average order value.
- Identify monthly and daily sales trends.
- Find the top-performing product lines and highest-revenue customers.
- Analyze sales performance across different cities and countries.
- Identify product lines with the highest number of cancelled orders.
- Generate actionable insights to support business decision-making.

## 📊 Analysis Performed

1. **Revenue Analysis:** Calculated total revenue from shipped orders.
2. **Order Performance:** Analyzed total orders, successful orders, and unsuccessful orders.
3. **Average Order Value:** Calculated average order value using the original SQL query.
4. **Monthly Sales Trend:** Analyzed monthly revenue patterns.
5. **Daily Sales Trend:** Evaluated daily sales performance.
6. **Product Performance:** Identified the top 5 product lines based on revenue.
7. **Customer Analysis:** Identified the top 10 customers contributing the highest revenue.
8. **City-wise Analysis:** Compared revenue generated across different cities.
9. **Country-wise Analysis:** Identified countries contributing the highest revenue.
10. **Cancellation Analysis:** Identified product lines with the highest number of cancelled orders.

## 💡 Key Business Insights

- **Total Sales:** Shipped orders generated approximately **$9.29 million** in sales.
- **Top Product Line:** Classic Cars generated the highest revenue of approximately **$3.70 million**.
- **Top Country:** The USA contributed the highest revenue of approximately **$3.37 million**.
- **Top Customer:** Euro Shopping Channel generated approximately **$741.5K** in revenue.
- **Cancelled Orders:** Ships recorded the highest number of cancellations, with **18 cancelled records**.

These insights help identify high-performing products, valuable customers, major revenue-generating markets, and areas that may require improvement.
